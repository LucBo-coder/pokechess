TACTICAL GUIDE
1 DEPLOYMENT: Select a Pokémon from your Bench and place it onto home ranks (Ranks 1 & 2). Spend Gold to expand your active squad up to 12 slots!
2 ELEMENT TYPES: Attacking from a White Tile strikes with Type 1. Attacking from a Black Tile strikes with Type 2. Defending takes damage based on both types (Super Effective x2, Resist 0.5x, or Immune 0x)!
3 EVOLUTION & STONES: Pokémon evolve after winning battles or using Evolution Stones (Fire, Water, Thunder, Moon...) from your Bag to unlock powerful forms!
4 TALISMANS & SYNERGIES: Deploy matching elements to activate TFT-style Synergy Tiers (3/6/9/12, 4/8/12, 2/3/4/5/6, etc.), and equip up to 3 talismans per Pokémon from the Shop!
